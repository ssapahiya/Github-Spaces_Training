# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Tech Leads / Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Solution Architects provide technical direction, define solution design, and ensure the implementation matches the product goals and long-term platform strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Guide engineering decisions and trade-offs
- Review implementation quality and technical debt
- Mentor developers and support technical growth
- Coordinate with Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility and sequencing
- Identify scalable and operationally sound approaches to delivery

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable technical solutions
- Reduce architectural drift and avoid rework
- Align implementation choices with product priorities and constraints
- Improve team engineering effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design review meetings
- Technical planning discussions with engineering and product leaders
- Design documents, ADRs, and technical decision logs

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation approaches and unblock technical decisions.
- Aligns with Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, quality, and delivery timelines.
- Supports Project Managers by identifying technical dependencies, risks, and delivery constraints.

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance and Test Leads define quality standards, shape test strategies, and help the team deliver reliable software that meets user and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality gates
- Design automated and manual test coverage for key workflows
- Track defects, risk, and quality trends
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria and edge-case validation
- Coordinate release validation and regression testing
- Report quality status to project stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce defects and production issues
- Improve confidence in each release
- Balance speed of delivery with quality expectations
- Ensure defects are clearly understood and resolved

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect triage and regression review meetings
- Release readiness discussions with engineering and product owners

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers to reproduce issues, validate fixes, and improve quality practices.
- Works with Product Managers to confirm user-facing requirements and acceptance criteria are appropriately tested.
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align release readiness, risk status, and testing timelines.

---

## DevOps / Release Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers enable reliable delivery by managing CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment readiness across environments.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain automation for build, test, and deployment
- Manage environments, infrastructure, and monitoring configuration
- Coordinate release plans and deployment execution
- Support service reliability, observability, and incident response
- Ensure access controls, secrets, and operational guardrails are in place
- Document deployment and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Support frequent, low-risk releases
- Improve deployment reliability and recovery time
- Reduce operational friction for engineering teams
- Maintain a secure and observable delivery environment

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment readiness reviews
- CI/CD and infrastructure status updates
- Post-release reviews and incident follow-ups

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to streamline delivery pipelines and deployment readiness.
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers to align release scope, timing, and stakeholder communication.
- Collaborates with Tech Leads on environment readiness and system health before launch.

---

## Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help teams improve their working practices, remove blockers, and maintain a healthy, high-performing delivery rhythm.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate ceremonies such as standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Remove or elevate impediments that slow the team
- Coach teams in Agile habits, collaboration, and continuous improvement
- Support sprint health, capacity planning, and team rhythm
- Guide process improvements that improve predictability and trust

### Goals
- Increase team effectiveness and predictability
- Strengthen collaboration and continuous learning
- Reduce friction caused by unclear process or organizational blockers
- Support sustainable team performance over time

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and retrospectives
- Team health check-ins and impediment tracking
- Coaching conversations with leaders and delivery teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers and Product Managers to keep planning and execution aligned with team capacity.
- Supports Project Managers in maintaining delivery cadence and managing cross-team dependencies.
- Helps Tech Leads and stakeholders improve decision-making and communication through healthier team rituals.

---

## Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors represent business goals, strategic priorities, and funding decisions. They provide direction for the project and sponsor its successful outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities and expected value
- Approve scope, budget, and strategic direction
- Resolve escalations and unblock organizational constraints
- Review project status and ensure alignment with strategic goals
- Support adoption and realization of outcomes after delivery

### Goals
- Deliver measurable business value and return on investment
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Enable informed decision-making and organizational support
- Ensure the project remains focused on outcomes, not just tasks

### Typical Communication
- Executive briefings and steering committee updates
- Business alignment reviews and resource decisions
- Risk escalation and milestone approvals

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides strategic direction to Product Managers and Project Managers.
- Reviews and prioritizes the work proposed by Product Managers and Tech Leads.
- Relies on Project Managers and Quality Leads for status, risk, and delivery confidence before major decisions.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help represent the broader project ecosystem involved in planning, delivery, quality, and governance.
- Clear role interactions help teams understand accountability, communication patterns, and collaboration across functions.
