# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the landing page for OctoAcme’s project management guidance. It gives the team a quick overview of the operating model and links to the detailed process documents in the `docs/` folder.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a structured project lifecycle that begins with project initiation and moves through planning, execution, release, and retrospective. At the start of each initiative, the team confirms the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and creates a lightweight one-pager to validate the project before significant work begins. Once approved, the team breaks work into a prioritized backlog, estimates scope, clarifies dependencies, and creates a release plan with clear milestones and acceptance criteria.

During execution, delivery is tracked through a regular rhythm of standups, planning, reviews, and status updates. The team uses project boards, PR workflows, CI checks, QA validation, and risk tracking to keep work visible and quality high. Communication is coordinated through documented stakeholder updates, escalation paths, and a single source of truth for status so teams and leaders stay aligned. Releases follow a controlled checklist that includes staging and smoke testing, rollback planning, stakeholder communication, and post-deploy verification to reduce operational risk.

Across the entire lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes clear roles and accountability. Project Managers coordinate schedules, dependencies, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and validate solutions; QA helps ensure quality and acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. After each sprint, milestone, or incident, the team conducts a retrospective to capture learnings, assign action items, and improve the process iteratively.
